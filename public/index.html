What these numbers mean

Download / Upload
How fast data moves to and from your device, in megabits per second. Measured with several parallel connections for 8 seconds each; the first 1.5 s (TCP ramp-up) is ignored.
Latency
Round-trip time to the test server while your connection is idle. Lower feels snappier.
Jitter
How much latency varies. High jitter hurts calls and games.
Bufferbloat
How much latency increases while your line is busy. A (under 30 ms) is great; D or F means video calls will stutter while someone downloads.

The test runs entirely in your browser against the selected server, so it measures your connection. Wi-Fi, VPNs and other devices on your network affect results.
